Current bag: Ethiopian washed, light roast, roasted 2 April. Delete this note when the bag is gone — none of it transfers to the next one.

Machine at 93C. Basket is the stock 18g. Grinder is the hand one, numbers are clicks from zero.

- **14 clicks, 18g in, 36g out** — 19 seconds. Gushed. Sour and thin, no crema to speak of.
- **12 clicks, 18g in, 36g out** — 24 seconds. Better but still sharp on the finish.
- **11 clicks, 18g in, 38g out** — 28 seconds. This is close. Floral, the acidity reads as lemon rather than as a fault.
- **10 clicks, 18g in, 38g out** — 33 seconds. Muddy, bitter tail. Too far.
- **11 clicks, 17.5g in, 38g out** — 29 seconds. Current setting. Slightly lower dose gives more room in the basket and the channelling stopped.

## Notes to self

Light roasts want a longer ratio than the 1:2 I default to. 1:2.2 was the fix here and I resisted it for a week for no reason.

The grinder drifts. Three clicks coarser than where I started on this bag, over five weeks, and the beans are also degassing, so I cannot separate the two effects. Retare the grinder at zero when the next bag opens.

Distribution mattered more than any grind change once I was in the right neighbourhood. The WDT tool eliminated the sour-and-bitter-in-the-same-cup problem entirely.

Water is filtered, not bottled. Have not tested whether it makes a difference and am not going to.
