## Conclusions so far

- Hydration between 75 and 76 per cent is the whole usable range for this flour. Below is dense, above is unmanageable by hand.
- Ambient temperature matters more than hydration and I ignored it for the first two months. Every degree above 20C takes roughly twenty minutes off the bulk.
- The hour of autolyse is worth more than any other single change I have made.
- Poke test beats timing. The clock entries above are descriptive, not prescriptive.

Next: try 10 per cent wholemeal at the same hydration and see whether the bulk shortens.
