Printed small and taped inside the lid of the camera bag, because the meter in the OM-1 is unreliable below about 4 EV and I never remember any of this at the moment I need it.

## Sunny sixteen

Shutter = 1/ISO, then set aperture by the light:

- f/16 — bright sun, hard-edged shadows
- f/11 — slight haze, soft-edged shadows
- f/8 — overcast, shadows barely visible
- f/5.6 — heavy overcast, no shadows
- f/4 — open shade, or sunset

So HP5 at 400: 1/500 at f/16 in full sun. Snow or beach, close down a stop. Backlit subject, open up two.

## Reciprocity failure

Past about one second the film stops responding linearly and the metered time is a lie. Rough corrections:

| Metered | HP5+ | Tri-X | Portra 400 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1s | 2s | 2s | 1s |
| 4s | 12s | 10s | 6s |
| 10s | 40s | 30s | 18s |
| 30s | 3min | 2.5min | 1.5min |

Colour films also shift while this happens — Portra warms, and past a minute it needs a weak cooling filter or you fix it in the scan.

## Push processing, HP5+ in DD-X (20°C)

- Box 400: 9 min
- Push to 800: 12 min
- Push to 1600: 17 min
- Push to 3200: 26 min, and accept the shadows are gone

Agitate ten seconds every minute. Pushing does not add shadow detail — it only moves the midtones up and stretches contrast, so meter for the highlights and let the blacks go.
