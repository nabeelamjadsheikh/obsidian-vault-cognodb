Decisions we have not made, written down so they stop being re-argued from scratch every time someone new joins the call. Each one gets a note on what would settle it, because "we should discuss this" is not a plan.

**Is Theme a node or a tag?** Settled by: whether anyone ever wants to link two themes to each other. If nobody does in three months of real use, it is a tag. The node-versus-property tension here is the same one worked through in [[Lantern — Data Model]], and I do not want to re-derive it.

**Can a highlight belong to two works?** Anthologies say yes, everything else says no. Settled by: the first real anthology import that breaks.

**What does a nearly-empty library see?** The unhappy path from [[Lantern — Onboarding Flow]]. Settled by: pick one of the three options and ship it; the argument has cost more than the feature will.

**Do we sync?** No, for now. Settled by: the first person who asks twice.

**Is the link view a graph at all?** I increasingly think it should be a ranked list with a small local diagram, not a canvas. Settled by: watching someone use it.

**What is the sharing story?** Unopened, deliberately, because opening it would expand the scope stated in [[Lantern — Project Overview]] past what two people can carry.

Keeping this file is itself a process change — it came out of [[Lantern — March Retro]], where we counted three separate meetings that had each rediscovered the Theme question independently. Anything that lands here should eventually leave, either into a decision or into the scope cut. Keiko has strong opinions on two of these; the ones she has already argued are in [[Design Review with Keiko]] and I have not moved them here to avoid duplicating the reasoning.
