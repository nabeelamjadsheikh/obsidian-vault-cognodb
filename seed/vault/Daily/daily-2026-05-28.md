Shipped the suggestion query this afternoon and it paid for itself within a minute of the first run, which almost never happens.

Two pairs at the top of the report:

1. A note on satisficing and a note on project scope. Same source, two shared tags, no link in either direction, sitting four folders apart since 2023. They are the same argument at two altitudes and I have been vaguely aware that one of them was incomplete without knowing why.
2. Two notes on interleaving that are not a missing link at all — they are a duplicate. Merged them tonight.

That second case is the one I did not design for. A very high overlap score does not always mean "link these", it sometimes means "you wrote this twice", and the report cannot tell the difference. Fine — a human reads twenty rows a week. The scoring and the inverse-frequency weighting are written up in [[Suggesting Links From Shared Entities]], and the weighting is the whole difference between a useful report and a list of everything tagged `software`.
